Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version: Vision Therapy Suite doesn’t collect, store or share any personal information. Everything happens on your device.

This policy covers the Vision Therapy Suite app for iPhone and iPad, published by Jeffrey Bentley (“I”, “me”).

Information the app collects

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party services or SDKs. It doesn’t connect to the internet to send data anywhere.

Information kept on your device

To work properly, the app saves a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • Your chart settings, such as grid size, letter height, level and metronome tempo.
  • The IDs of your recent charts, such as V3L-1042, so you can reopen them.

None of this identifies you or your patients, and it never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Patient names and dates

When printing or sharing, you can type a name and date to appear on the sheets. That text is placed on the PDF you create and isn’t saved by the app. Once you close the print or share screen, it’s gone.

Printing and sharing

The app creates PDFs on your device. When you print or share one, it goes to the printer, app or person you choose, through Apple’s standard print and share features. What happens to it after that is governed by that service and by you.

Children

The app doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices ever change, I’ll update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
