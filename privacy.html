Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The short version: Vision Therapy Suite doesn’t collect, store or share any personal information. Everything happens on your device.

This policy covers the Vision Therapy Suite app for iPhone and iPad, published by Jeffrey Bentley (“I”, “me”).

Information the app collects

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party services or SDKs. It doesn’t connect to the internet. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Information kept on your device

To work properly, the app saves a few things in its own private storage on your device:

  • Your chart settings, such as grid size, character height, font, level, paper size and metronome tempo.
  • Your Present mode choices, such as reading pattern, highlight style and light or dark.
  • The codes of your recent charts, such as V8JQ9W, so you can reopen them.
  • Your packet cover instructions, if you edit them.
  • Your clinic name and logo, if you add them, so they can print on your sheets.

None of this identifies your patients, and it never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Your clinic logo

When you choose a logo, you pick it with Apple’s Photos or Files picker, so the app only gets the one image you choose and never sees the rest of your library. It’s scaled down and stored in the app’s private storage. It’s never uploaded and never included in chart codes. You can change or remove it at any time in About.

Patient names and dates

When printing, you can type a name and date to appear on the sheets. That text is placed on the PDF and isn’t saved by the app. Chart codes never contain names or dates.

Printing and PDFs

The app creates PDFs on your device and prints them through Apple’s standard print options (AirPrint). If you save or send a PDF from the print options, it goes to the app, place or person you choose, and what happens after that is governed by that service and by you. The app clears its own temporary copies of PDFs automatically.

Children

The app doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices ever change, I’ll update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email visiontherapysuite@gmail.com.

Vision Therapy Suite makes printable worksheets for vision therapy done under the direction of an eye care professional. It doesn’t diagnose, measure or treat anything.
