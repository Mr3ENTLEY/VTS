Frequently asked questions

My printed letters are the wrong size.

Print from the app with the Print button. If you print a saved PDF from another device, use 100% scale and 1 page per sheet. Pages per Sheet shrinks everything. Each sheet has a 50 mm bar in the footer: measure it with a ruler. If it isn’t 50 mm, your printer is scaling the page.

How do I print the same chart again?

Every sheet has a chart ID in its footer, such as V8JQ9W or S2MBE. In the app, tap the clock icon, choose Open Chart ID and type it in. You’ll get exactly the same chart.

How do I make a home practice packet?

On Home, tap a 5, 10 or 20 pack under either tool. Or open the Share tab in Visual Chart or Slap Tap and choose the number of sheets, then tap Print. Each sheet is a new chart, and you can add a cover page with instructions, a metronome target and a daily log.

Can I show a chart on a TV or iPad screen?

Tap the play icon at the top of either generator to open Present mode. Swipe left for a new chart and right to go back. Use Screen Mirroring to show it on a TV.

Where does the app store my patients’ information?

It doesn’t. Names typed onto a sheet are printed and never saved. See the Privacy Policy.

Vision Therapy Suite makes printable worksheets for vision therapy done under the direction of an eye care professional. It doesn’t diagnose, measure or treat anything.
