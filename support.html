Frequently asked questions

My printed letters are the wrong size.

Print with the Print button in the app. It always prints at 100%. If you print a saved PDF from a computer, choose Actual size and one page per sheet; Fit to page or Pages per sheet will shrink it. Every sheet has a 50 mm bar: measure it with a ruler. If it isn’t 50 mm, your printer is scaling the page.

How do I save or email a PDF?

Tap Print, then use the share button at the top of the print options. From there you can save to Files, AirDrop it or email it.

Can I print on A4 paper?

Yes. Open the Share tab in Visual Chart or Slap Tap and choose Letter or A4. Charts resize to fit the page.

How do I print the same chart again?

Every sheet has a chart code in its footer, such as V8JQ9W or S2MBE. In the app, tap the clock icon, choose Open Chart ID and type it in. You get exactly the same chart, settings and all. Tap the code at the top of the screen to copy it. Codes from older versions still open.

How do I make a home practice packet?

On Home, tap a 5, 10 or 20 pack under either tool. Or open the Share tab and choose the number of sheets, then tap Print. Each sheet is a new chart, and packets can include a cover page with your instructions, a metronome target for Slap Tap and a practice log.

How do I add my clinic name and logo?

Open About and fill in the Clinic section. Choose a logo from Photos or Files. With the Logo tag on in the Share tab, your name and logo print in each sheet’s footer and on packet covers. Both stay on your device.

How do I change the character size?

In Visual Chart, open the Characters tab and use the slider or the − and + buttons. Choose mm, pt or px. You can also swipe up or down on the preview to jump to the next multiple of 5.

What are the Slap Tap levels?

Level A uses the single symbols b, d, p and q. Level B adds the doubles qp, db, pb and qd. Level C adds rests, shown as O.

How does the metronome work?

Open the Metronome tab in Slap Tap. Tempo goes up to 150 BPM. Play counts in one bar of 4 clicks, then clicks on every beat. The same tempo carries into Present mode and can print on packet covers.

Can I show a chart on a TV or iPad screen?

Tap the play button at the top of either tool to open Present mode. Swipe left for the next chart and right to go back; arrow keys and clickers work too. The options button sets the reading pattern, highlight style and colour, and light or dark. Use Screen Mirroring to show it on a TV.

Does it work on iPad and without internet?

Yes. Vision Therapy Suite runs on iPhone and iPad and works fully offline.

Where does the app store my patients’ information?

It doesn’t. Names and dates typed onto a sheet are printed and never saved. See the Privacy Policy.
