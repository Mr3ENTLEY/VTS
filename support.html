Frequently asked questions

My printed letters are the wrong size.

In the print dialog, use 100% scale and 1 page per sheet. Pages per Sheet shrinks everything. Each sheet has a 50 mm bar in the footer: measure it with a ruler. If it isn’t 50 mm, your printer is scaling the page.

How do I print the same chart again?

Every sheet has a chart ID in its footer, such as V3L-1042 or S2-1042. In the app, tap the clock icon, choose Open Chart ID and type it in. You’ll get exactly the same chart.

How do I make a home practice packet?

Tap Print or Share and choose 5, 10 or 20 sheets. Each sheet is a new chart, and you can add a cover page with instructions, a metronome target and a daily log.

What are near cards?

In Visual Chart, tap Print or Share and turn on Add near cards. You get a page of small cards with the same letters as the wall chart, sized for the distances you choose, with dashed lines to cut along.

Can I show a chart on a TV or iPad screen?

Tap the play icon at the top of either generator to open Present mode. Swipe left for a new chart and right to go back. Use Screen Mirroring to show it on a TV.

Where does the app store my patients’ information?

It doesn’t. Names typed onto a sheet are printed and never saved. See the Privacy Policy.
