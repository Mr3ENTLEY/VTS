Updates

What's new in each version. Update from the App Store to get the latest.

Version 1.2

Latest

November 16, 2026

  • One button to print. Share is gone and Print opens at true size. To save or send the PDF, use the share button in the print options.
  • Slap Tap sheets now show the title, chart code and 50 mm ruler at the top of the page.
  • Room for a hole punch along the top edge of Slap Tap pages and packet covers.
  • A cleaner Home and About page.
  • Under-the-hood tidying for speed and reliability.

Version 1.1

November 14, 2026

  • True-size printing straight from the app. Every page prints at 100%, so the 50 mm bar measures 50 mm.
  • Quick print on Home: 1 sheet or a 5, 10 or 20 pack in two taps.
  • Your clinic's name and logo on every sheet and packet cover. Set them in About.
  • Present mode options: reading patterns, highlight style and colour, light or dark, and swipe up or down to resize.
  • Character height moves in whole steps with the slider and buttons. Swipe the preview to jump to the next multiple of 5.
  • Every font now uses the same spacing, grid lines match the border, and border spacing has more room.
  • Shorter chart codes. Codes from 1.0 still open.
  • A4 paper alongside US Letter.
  • A short guide for each feature in About.

Version 1.0

November 10, 2026

  • Visual Chart: 3×3 or 4×4 letter and number charts at a true, measurable size.
  • Slap Tap: b, d, p and q sheets in three levels, with a built-in metronome.
  • Chart codes on every sheet, so you can bring back the exact same chart.
  • Packets of 5, 10 or 20 sheets with a cover page and practice log.
  • Present mode for tablets and mirrored TVs.

Vision Therapy Suite makes printable worksheets for vision therapy done under the direction of an eye care professional. It doesn’t diagnose, measure or treat anything.
